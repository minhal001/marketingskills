# Elumind Centres for Brain Excellence - Marketing Plan

## Company Overview

**Business**: Private outpatient mental health center specializing in brain optimization through neurotherapeutic modalities (Neurofeedback, TMS, QEEG Brain Mapping, Biofeedback, Photobiomodulation, Psychotherapy, Functional Medicine).

**Locations**: North Vancouver (HQ), Kelowna, Langley (British Columbia, Canada)

**Founded**: 2018 by Dr. Kourosh Edalati

**Differentiator**: Integrated, personalized, case-managed approach combining advanced neuroscience technologies with holistic therapies -- not just talk therapy or just medication, but a full-spectrum brain health solution.

**Core Services**: QEEG Brain Mapping, Neurofeedback, TMS, Biofeedback, Photobiomodulation, Psychotherapy (EMDR, CBT, Clinical Hypnosis), Life Coaching, Functional Medicine, Nutrition Counselling, Sleep Program, Pain Program, Smoking Cessation.

**Conditions Treated**: ADHD, Anxiety, Depression, PTSD, OCD, Autism, Chronic Pain, TBI, Sleep Disorders, Migraines, Memory/Dementia, Fibromyalgia, Post-Stroke.

---

## Target Audiences

### Primary Audiences

**1. Adults with Treatment-Resistant Mental Health Conditions (Ages 30-55)**
- Have tried medication and/or traditional therapy without sufficient results
- Searching for alternatives to medication-only approaches
- Higher income, willing to pay out-of-pocket for private care
- Search terms: "neurofeedback for anxiety," "TMS for depression," "alternatives to antidepressants," "brain mapping near me"

**2. Parents of Children with ADHD/Autism (Ages 30-50)**
- Looking for non-medication alternatives for their children
- Concerned about long-term medication side effects
- Active in parent communities and forums
- Search terms: "ADHD treatment without medication," "neurofeedback for ADHD kids," "autism therapy Vancouver"

**3. High-Performing Professionals Seeking Brain Optimization (Ages 25-45)**
- Not necessarily diagnosed with a condition
- Want peak cognitive performance, better sleep, stress management
- Tech-savvy, wellness-oriented, open to biohacking
- Search terms: "brain optimization," "neurofeedback for performance," "improve focus and concentration"

### Secondary Audiences

**4. Referring Clinicians** (Psychiatrists, GPs, Psychologists)
- Need confidence in clinical outcomes and credentials
- Want a reliable referral partner for patients who need more than standard care

**5. Corporate Wellness / HR Leaders**
- Looking for mental health benefits for employees
- Interested in brain health programs as a perk

### 3. Content Marketing & Thought Leadership

**Priority: HIGH** -- Build authority in a field where trust is everything.

**Blog Content Calendar (Monthly)**
- 2 searchable blog posts (condition/treatment-focused, SEO-optimized)
- 1 shareable blog post (patient story, research insight, or thought leadership)
- 1 "explainer" post demystifying a treatment (e.g., "What Happens During a QEEG Brain Map?")

**Video Content**
- Treatment explainer videos (2-3 min) for each core service: walk through what a neurofeedback session, TMS session, or brain mapping appointment looks like

- Dr. Edalati thought leadership clips on brain health topics
- Post natively on YouTube, Instagram Reels, and TikTok

**Shareable Content Ideas**
- "The Roads, Cars, and Drivers" framework explained visually (Elumind's brain health analogy)
- Original data: "What We've Learned from 1,000+ Brain Maps" (anonymized insights)
- Myth-busting: "5 Things Most People Get Wrong About Neurofeedback"

**E-E-A-T Optimization (Critical for Health Content)**
- Author bios on every blog post with credentials (MD, FRCPC, PhD, etc.)
- Link to published research where applicable
- Include "Medically reviewed by Dr. [Name]" badges
- Reference peer-reviewed studies in all clinical content

### 4. Email Marketing

**Priority: MEDIUM** -- Nurture leads who aren't ready to book immediately.

**Lead Magnets to Build the List**
1. "The Complete Guide to Brain Mapping: What It Reveals About Your Mental Health" (PDF)
2. "Is Neurofeedback Right for You? A Self-Assessment Checklist"
3. "5 Drug-Free Approaches to Treating [Anxiety/ADHD/Depression]" (condition-specific)

**Email Sequences**

**Sequence 1: New Lead Nurture (Post-Download)**
```
Email 1 (Immediate): Deliver lead magnet + introduce Elumind's approach
Email 2 (Day 2): "The science behind brain mapping" - educational value
Email 3 (Day 4): Patient success story relevant to their condition
Email 4 (Day 7): "What to expect at your first visit" - reduce friction
Email 5 (Day 10): Address common objections (cost, time, "does it really work?")
Email 6 (Day 14): Direct CTA - book a free 15-minute consultation
```

**Sequence 2: Post-Consultation (Didn't Book Treatment)**
```
Email 1 (Day 1): Thank you + summary of what was discussed
Email 2 (Day 3): Relevant case study / testimonial
Email 3 (Day 7): "Questions we commonly hear after consultations" (FAQ)
Email 4 (Day 14): Gentle follow-up with booking link
```

**Sequence 3: Monthly Newsletter**
- New research/insights in brain health
- Patient spotlight (with consent)
- Clinic news and new services
- Helpful tips (sleep hygiene, stress management, brain exercises)

### 5. Social Media

**Priority: MEDIUM** -- Build awareness and trust; not a primary conversion channel.

**Platform Focus**
- **Instagram**: Primary social channel. Visual content of clinic, team, treatment rooms. Reels explaining treatments. Patient stories (with consent). Brain health tips.
- **YouTube**: Long-form treatment explainers, patient journeys, Dr. Edalati interviews. Strong SEO value.
- **LinkedIn**: Clinician referral network. Share research, thought leadership. Target referring healthcare professionals.
- **Facebook**: Local community engagement. Parent groups (ADHD/autism). Event promotion.

**Content Mix**
- 40% educational (brain health facts, treatment explainers, myth-busting)
- 30% social proof (patient stories, reviews, awards, clinic milestones)
- 20% behind-the-scenes (team introductions, clinic tours, day-in-the-life)
- 10% direct promotion (consultation offers, new service announcements)

### 6. Referral Program

**Priority: MEDIUM** -- High-trust industry where word-of-mouth is powerful.

**Patient Referral Program**
- **Incentive**: Double-sided -- referring patient receives a credit toward a future session, referred patient gets a discount on their initial assessment
- **Trigger moments**: After completing a treatment milestone, after a positive outcome check-in, after leaving a review
- **Mechanism**: Personalized referral link or simple referral card (physical + digital)
- **Goal**: 25% of new patients from referrals within 12 months

**Clinician Referral Program**
- Build a formal referral network with local GPs, psychiatrists, psychologists, and pediatricians
- Provide referring clinicians with outcome data and clinical summaries (with patient consent)
- Host quarterly "Lunch and Learn" sessions for referral partners to educate them on neurofeedback, TMS, and brain mapping
- Create a dedicated clinician referral portal on the website (elumind.com already has this at /clinicians-and-providers/)

### 7. Website Conversion Optimization

**Priority: HIGH** -- The website is the primary conversion point.

**Homepage CRO**
- Headline should communicate the core value proposition immediately: what Elumind does, for whom, and why it's different
- Primary CTA above the fold: "Book Your Free 15-Minute Consultation"
- Add trust signals near the CTA: Healthcare Business Review award badge, number of patients treated, number of brain maps completed
- Include a "How It Works" section: (1) Book a free consultation, (2) Get your personalized brain map, (3) Start your treatment plan
- Add condition-specific entry points: "I'm looking for help with: [Anxiety] [ADHD] [Depression] [Sleep] [Other]" -- routes visitors to relevant service pages

**Service Pages**
- Each condition and treatment should have a dedicated landing page
- Structure: Problem description (in patient language) -> How Elumind's approach works -> What to expect -> Outcomes/evidence -> Patient testimonials -> CTA to book consultation
- Add FAQ sections with schema markup

**Booking Flow**
- Minimize friction: reduce form fields to name, email, phone, condition of interest, preferred location
- Offer online scheduling tool (Calendly, Acuity, or similar) for self-service booking
- Add live chat or chatbot for immediate questions during business hours

## Competitive Positioning

Elumind's marketing should consistently reinforce these differentiators:

1. **Integrated approach**: Not just one treatment modality -- a comprehensive, personalized plan combining neuroscience technology with holistic therapies
2. **Brain-first diagnosis**: Starts with objective QEEG brain mapping, not just symptom checklists
3. **Proven technology**: Clinically validated, peer-reviewed treatments (not experimental or fringe)
4. **Recognized excellence**: "Top Mental Health Services Provider" -- Healthcare Business Review
5. **Personalized care**: Case-managed treatment plans tailored to each individual's brain patterns
6. **Drug-free options**: Alternatives to medication-only approaches (a major differentiator for many patients)

## Risk Considerations

- **Healthcare advertising regulations**: All ad copy and content must comply with Canadian healthcare advertising standards. Avoid making claims that overstate efficacy. Use language like "may help with" rather than "cures" or "treats."
- **Patient privacy**: All testimonials, case studies, and patient stories require explicit written consent. Anonymize clinical data used in content.
- **Review management**: Negative reviews require prompt, professional, PHIPA-compliant responses. Never disclose patient information in review responses.
- **Content accuracy**: All clinical content should be reviewed by a licensed clinician before publication to ensure medical accuracy.
