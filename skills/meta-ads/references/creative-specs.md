# Meta Ads Creative Specs & Templates

Complete creative specifications and best practices for Meta ad placements.

## Ad Format Specs

### Image Ads

| Placement | Aspect Ratio | Resolution | File Type | Max Size |
|-----------|-------------|------------|-----------|----------|
| Feed (FB/IG) | 1:1 | 1080x1080 | JPG, PNG | 30MB |
| Feed (FB/IG) | 4:5 | 1080x1350 | JPG, PNG | 30MB |
| Stories/Reels | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | JPG, PNG | 30MB |
| Right Column (FB) | 1.91:1 | 1200x628 | JPG, PNG | 30MB |
| Marketplace | 1:1 | 1080x1080 | JPG, PNG | 30MB |
| Search Results | 1.91:1 | 1200x628 | JPG, PNG | 30MB |

### Video Ads

| Placement | Aspect Ratio | Resolution | Length | File Type | Max Size |
|-----------|-------------|------------|--------|-----------|----------|
| Feed | 1:1 or 4:5 | 1080x1080+ | Up to 240 min | MP4, MOV | 4GB |
| Stories/Reels | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Up to 60 sec | MP4, MOV | 4GB |
| In-stream | 16:9 | 1920x1080 | 5-15 sec | MP4, MOV | 4GB |
| Reels (organic style) | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | 15-30 sec | MP4, MOV | 4GB |

**Video best practices:**
- First 3 seconds must hook attention
- Design for sound-off (add captions always)
- Vertical (9:16) for Stories/Reels, square (1:1) or 4:5 for feed
- Keep under 30 seconds for best completion rates
- 15 seconds is the sweet spot for most objectives

### Carousel Ads

- 2-10 cards per carousel
- Each card: 1080x1080 (1:1) image or video
- Each card gets its own headline and description
- First card is most important (hook)
- Last card should have strong CTA

### Collection Ads

- Cover image/video + product catalog
- Cover: 1080x1080 (1:1) or 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Pulls products automatically from catalog
- Best for e-commerce with 4+ products

## Copy Character Limits

| Field | Limit | Recommended | Visible |
|-------|-------|-------------|---------|
| Primary text | 125 chars before truncation | 80-100 chars | First ~125 chars, then "See more" |
| Headline | 40 chars recommended | 25-40 chars | Below image/video |
| Description | 30 chars recommended | 20-30 chars | Below headline, not always shown |
| URL display | Auto-generated | N/A | Domain shown automatically |

**Primary text tips:**
- Front-load the hook — first line determines engagement
- Long-form (3-5 sentences) can work for complex products
- Short-form (1-2 sentences) for simple offers
- Test both lengths

## Creative Types That Work

### 1. UGC-Style Video

Looks like organic content, not an ad.

**Structure:**
1. Person talks to camera (0-3 sec hook)
2. States the problem (3-8 sec)
3. Shows the product/solution (8-20 sec)
4. Results/reaction (20-25 sec)
5. CTA (25-30 sec)

**Tips:**
- Film on a phone, not a professional camera
- Natural lighting, casual setting
- Authentic enthusiasm, not scripted-sounding
- Captions are mandatory

### 2. Problem-Solution Static

Single image that shows the problem and your solution.

**Layout options:**
- Split screen: "Before vs. After"
- Top/bottom: Problem (top) → Solution (bottom)
- Single image with bold text overlay

**Tips:**
- Keep text under 20% of image area (not required, but performs better)
- Bold, readable font
- High contrast between text and background
- One clear focal point

### 3. Social Proof Ad

Builds trust through testimonials, numbers, or logos.

**Variations:**
- Customer quote as primary text + product image
- Screenshot of review/tweet
- "Trusted by X companies" with logo bar
- Before/after results with real numbers

### 4. Carousel Storytelling

Each card advances a narrative or shows a different benefit.

**Structures:**
- Problem → Feature 1 → Feature 2 → Feature 3 → CTA
- Myth 1 → Myth 2 → Myth 3 → Truth → Your solution
- Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Result
- Benefit 1 → Benefit 2 → Benefit 3 → Testimonial → CTA

### 5. Founder/Team Story

Personal story that humanizes the brand.

**Works well for:**
- D2C brands
- Startups
- Products with a strong origin story
- Audiences that value authenticity

### 6. Product Demo

Short video showing the product in action.

**Structure:**
1. Hook with the outcome (0-3 sec)
2. Quick walkthrough of key features (3-20 sec)
3. Show the result (20-25 sec)
4. CTA (25-30 sec)

**Tips:**
- Screen recording with voiceover for software
- Close-up product shots for physical products
- Speed up boring parts
- Highlight the "aha moment"

## Creative Testing Framework

### Phase 1: Concept Testing

Test 3-5 completely different angles/concepts:
- Problem-focused vs. outcome-focused
- Emotional vs. rational
- Social proof vs. direct response
- UGC vs. polished
- Short vs. long copy

**Budget:** $20-30/day per ad
**Duration:** 3-5 days
**Kill criteria:** CPA > 2x target after 1,000+ impressions

### Phase 2: Winner Iteration

Take the winning concept and create variations:
- Different hooks (first 3 seconds of video, first line of text)
- Different visuals (same message, different imagery)
- Different CTAs
- Different copy lengths

**Budget:** $30-50/day per ad
**Duration:** 5-7 days
**Goal:** Find the best-performing variation

### Phase 3: Format Expansion

Adapt the winning creative to different formats:
- Static → video
- Video → carousel
- Long-form → short-form
- Feed → Stories/Reels

### Creative Refresh Cadence

| Spend Level | Refresh Frequency | Signals to Refresh |
|-------------|------------------|-------------------|
| < $5K/month | Every 4-6 weeks | Frequency > 3, CTR dropping |
| $5-20K/month | Every 2-4 weeks | Frequency > 2.5, CPA rising |
| $20K+/month | Every 1-2 weeks | Frequency > 2, performance decline |

## Ad Copy Templates

### For Awareness/Top of Funnel

**The Question Hook:**
```
Still [doing painful thing] every [time period]?

There's a better way. [Product] helps you [outcome] without [pain point].

See how it works →
```

**The Stat Lead:**
```
[X]% of [audience] struggle with [problem].

We built [Product] so you don't have to. [Key benefit] in [timeframe].

Learn more →
```

### For Consideration/Middle of Funnel

**The Social Proof:**
```
"[Customer quote about results]" — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

[Product] helps [audience] [achieve outcome]. Join [X]+ teams already using it.

Try it free →
```

**The Feature-Benefit:**
```
[Feature name] means you can finally [desirable outcome].

No more [pain point]. No more [pain point]. Just [positive result].

[Product] — [one-line value prop].

Start free trial →
```

### For Conversion/Bottom of Funnel

**The Urgency:**
```
Your [trial/cart/offer] is waiting.

[Reiterate key benefit]. [Address top objection].

[CTA with specificity] →
```

**The Objection Handler:**
```
"But what about [common objection]?"

We get it. That's why [Product] includes [feature that addresses objection].

[Proof point]. [CTA] →
```

## Placement-Specific Tips

### Instagram Feed
- Visual quality matters more than Facebook
- Carousel posts get high engagement
- Keep text minimal on images
- Use lifestyle imagery when possible

### Instagram Stories/Reels
- Full-screen vertical (9:16) is mandatory
- First 1-2 seconds must hook — people swipe fast
- Use native Stories features (text, stickers) for organic feel
- Poll and question stickers boost engagement
- Keep to 15 seconds or under for Reels

### Facebook Feed
- Longer primary text can work (people still read on Facebook)
- Link posts with clear headlines perform well
- Video autoplay catches attention
- Comments and social proof visible (encourage engagement)

### Audience Network
- Lower quality traffic, but cheap reach
- Use for awareness/reach campaigns only
- Exclude from conversion campaigns unless performance is good
- Monitor placement-level CPA closely

### Messenger
- Best for retargeting with personalized messages
- Click-to-Messenger ads for sales conversations
- Sponsored messages for re-engagement
- Keep conversation flows short and action-oriented
